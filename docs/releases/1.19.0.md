# Ingredient Check 1.19.0

Orders pages show individual food scores even when purchase-history import is off. The large
paragraph above the order list is replaced by the existing two buttons and, when saved rated
purchases exist, a compact inline history graph. Daily, weekly and monthly averages appear only
for periods with eligible ratings. Today, the Monday-based current week and the calendar month
use the browser's local calendar. Missing, returned and cancelled ratings do not enter averages.

Saving purchase records and account sync remain separate opt-in features. With automatic rating
off, Orders shows saved readings only. Public missing-label lookups omit cookies and referrers.
Changing comparison goals does not trigger another history import or rewrite recorded snapshots.

## Shopping improvements

- **Check a label · Saved & compare** in the popup opens a standalone label checker. Paste text,
  read a local photo, or explicitly look up a barcode. OCR text is reviewed before scoring, and
  an explicit barcode lookup does not change the automatic lookup preference.
- **Save product** on Amazon and retailer product panels saves bounded label evidence into a
  durable local shortlist. Named lists survive cache clearing. Saved entries can be removed
  independently; they are not added to Google settings backup or purchase sync.
- **Detailed comparisons** show current-goal scores, source/freshness, selected warning matches,
  unassessed factors, normalized nutrient amounts and the main point contributions. Goal previews
  do not change settings or stored evidence. Matching prices from the current Amazon comparison
  session are shown as search prices; missing prices stay unavailable.
- **Comparison filters** constrain maximum listing price, minimum score, evidence completeness
  and selected warning matches before ranking. Unknown warning checks have an explicit include
  choice. Filtered results are not a claim of allergen absence.
- **Evidence summaries** travel through local score caches and ranked results. Older cached
  answers without evidence stay unknown. Source, capture date, partial readings and missing
  selected nutrition data are visible without changing the numerical preference score.
- **Allergen evidence** distinguishes a listed ingredient, a contains declaration, cross-contact
  warning and other advisory. Mixed declarations retain the matching source text. The same
  warning matches and numerical rules are retained; the scoring revision changes to retire
  older records that lack the updated explanation contract.

## Distribution and coverage

Both browser packages can be built from this source. Chrome Web Store publication and permanent
Firefox distribution still require publisher submission and Mozilla signing. No publication or
new live-retailer support claim is made. Existing retailer previews retain their status and are
included in the controlled regression harness.

See [the implementation/validation record](../FEATURE-INTEGRATION-2026-10-30.md) and
[privacy details](../PRIVACY.md). Personal-care indices remain separate from food scores.
