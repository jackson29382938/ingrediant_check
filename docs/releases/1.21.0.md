# 1.21.0

- Walmart search has a collapsible **Price & health** drawer on the right. It uses the same ranking and unit-price engines as Amazon, with Healthiest, Lowest price, Lowest price per oz and Balanced presets, plus health/unit-price importance sliders. Walmart's own sort and filter controls remain intact.
- The drawer reserves space on desktop so the retailer page resizes to its left, including fixed headers/widgets. Closing or pausing restores the page width. On narrow screens the comparison appears below the results in normal document flow, without covering them.
- The comparison includes only discovered organic results. It updates as public labels arrive, shares the existing two-at-a-time label queue, and marks unknown scores/prices rather than inventing them. Repeated cards share one comparison entry. Mixed categories disable health ranking.
- Read exact accessible Walmart prices rather than its visually split dollars/cents. Convert weight-based unit prices to dollars per ounce, so different package sizes compare consistently. Other unit families are not compared as ounces.
- Search grids compact around hidden sponsored cards, and empty promotional slots collapse. Cards stay in their original DOM order and React containers. Pause restores the original display/layout and removes the drawer; dynamic cards and filters remain observed.
- Live Chromium screenshots confirm a compact four-column grid, working health/price/unit-price ranking, and a usable mobile drawer. Content scripts were injected directly into live Walmart with simulated extension storage/runtime APIs; the managed browser still prohibits installing the unpacked extension.

Compaction succeeded on the tested live pages, so the conditional whitespace-stat fillers were not needed. The final partially populated row of a result container may naturally remain incomplete; this is not a reserved ad slot.
