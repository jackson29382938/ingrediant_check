# 1.20.1

- Walmart search hides sponsored product cards and sponsored modules, including dynamically inserted ads. Pausing Ingredient Check restores the page.
- Walmart search automatically rates organic results while Ingredient Check is enabled, even when the global result badges or automatic-rating switches are off. Every discovered result can be rated; requests remain limited to two at a time and stop on access denial or rate limiting.
- Support Walmart cards identified by item ID or product-card/list-view containers, results within forms, late card updates, and trailing-slash search URLs.
- Missing or blocked ingredient declarations show “Label unavailable”; numeric scores still require a readable published label. No rating is inferred from a product title.

Validation uses intercepted public-page fixtures and the real scoring engine. Live Walmart search and unpacked-extension installation are not verified in the managed cloud browser.
