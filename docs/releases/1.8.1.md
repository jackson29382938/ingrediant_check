# Ingredient Check 1.8.1 — grocery search and product fixes

Safeway and Albertsons food products now run on their modern `/shop/pd/<product-name>/<id>` routes. Their public embedded declarations are parsed without executing scripts and are accepted only when the catalog product, URL, canonical and page wrapper agree. Published nested nutrition quantities are read; unavailable products, conflicting declarations, unresolved references and missing serving weights remain unknown.

QFC, Harris Teeter and Dillons were checked first, followed by Kroger, Fry’s, Ralphs, King Soopers, Smith’s and Fred Meyer. Across these eleven grocery banners, search requests preserve the actual same-origin product/fulfillment link. Badges sit outside truncated title links and cart controls, numbered product cards are recognized, and trailing-slash searches receive the extension. A changed visible declaration cannot borrow the old embedded declaration’s nutrition or advisories.

The separately maintained Amazon, Target, Walmart, Costco and Instacart adapters/pipelines are unchanged.

## Validation

- 965 Node tests, zero-warning lint, preflight and packaging.
- Actual unpacked-extension Chrome checks for all eleven banners: cold-cache and cached ratings, appended results, SPA search/product navigation, changed declarations, recycled product cards, unavailable labels and unclipped badge geometry.
- Existing Amazon, retailer, popup, setup, backup, Appearance and accessibility browser regressions remain included.
- Fresh ordinary TLS-verified public product/search requests are documented in the [bottom-five audit](../BOTTOM-FIVE-AUDIT-2026-10-06.md) and [remaining grocery audit](../GROCERY-SEARCH-AUDIT-2026-10-06.md).

All grocery banners remain previews. Direct live Chrome checks are still blocked by the cloud environment’s certificate trust; controlled browser tests and successful public GETs do not establish coverage of every live layout. Numerical scores require readable published labels, and the configured search request budget still applies.

## Updating

Replace the files in your existing unpacked extension folder, click Reload at `chrome://extensions`, then refresh shopping tabs. Keeping the existing folder preserves the extension ID and Google redirect address. No new store domains or host permissions are added.
