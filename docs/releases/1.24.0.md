# 1.24.0

- Food products on public Walmart, Target, and Amazon browse pages now receive Ingredient Check ratings, including filtered Walmart savings pages such as Rollback Food.
- Ratings also appear in product carousels and recommendations on supported retailer pages, with card identity and same-origin links preserved where a carousel omits a direct product identifier.
- Walmart and Target responsive listing layouts are handled consistently, and Amazon Fresh and Deals cards are covered by the same food eligibility and rating flow.
- Food-card processing is paced and stops cleanly at retailer human-verification challenges; ads and non-product cards are not rated.
- The food eligibility cache revision was advanced so the updated Scoops tortilla/corn-chip handling applies when listings are rated again.

Validation: full unit and serial test suites (1,368 passing), ESLint, retailer browser checks, and the CI harness passed. Live Walmart Rollback, Target Snacks, Amazon Fresh, Amazon search, and product-recommendation checks were exercised; Walmart's human-verification page returned unavailable labels after the challenge, while eligible cards were still detected and processed.
