# 1.24.0

- Walmart and Target: new **Sort results** panel on search pages. Its first and default order is **Price per unit,
  low to high**, priced per ounce, fluid ounce or item, whichever each result states or its title gives
  unambiguously. A *Price per* picker (Automatic, oz, lb, 100 g, kg, fl oz, pt, qt, gal, 100 ml, L, item) re-ranks the
  list; pounds and ounces convert, weight, volume and item counts never do, and results that cannot be priced in the
  chosen unit stay last, marked *No unit price*. Healthiest, Lowest price and Balanced orders and the two importance
  sliders remain. The panel replaces Walmart's 1.21.0 "Price & health" drawer and keeps its docking beside the page;
  thumbnails and rank numbers are new, and the choice and open state persist across searches in the tab.
- Fix: **the extension did not run for a search started from the Walmart or Target home page.** Both sites change the
  address without loading a page, and the script was injected only on results and product pages, so a search typed
  into the header got no ratings, no sort and every advertisement. It now also loads on the public home and
  category/browse routes (see `docs/PRIVACY.md`); account, cart, checkout and login pages stay out.
- Fix: **Walmart sponsored results were not always hidden.** A banner marked only by its "Sponsored" label and an ad
  whose only link is Walmart's ad tracker were missed even after a reload. Ads are now recognised by any of several
  independent signals (ad-tracker link, placement attribute, accessible name ending in "Sponsored", the label itself),
  hidden as the outermost wrapper that holds only that ad, and never rated or fetched. Ads that arrive after load are
  hidden on the next frame; organic results, the grid and the page are never hidden. Pausing restores every ad.
- Hidden-ad bookkeeping no longer un-hides and re-hides every ad on each pass; a full pass on a 221-card page costs
  about a quarter of what it did.
- Target: advertisements are tagged *Sponsored* in the sort panel (they are not hidden).
- Scores, scoring rules and `RULES_REVISION` are unchanged.

Known limits: Walmart's and Target's live search pages could not be captured (Walmart's bot check, Target HTTP 429);
the browser checks use replica pages built from earlier recordings. See `docs/STORES.md`.

Validation: unit tests (`tests/walmart-sponsored.test.js`, `tests/retailer-sort.test.js`), preflight, ESLint, and
`npm run retailers:check`, which drives the real unpacked extension through search-from-home, ad hiding, every sort
order and unit, the cart and a phone width on both stores, with screenshots in `docs/screenshots/retailers/`.
