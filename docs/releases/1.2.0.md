Scoring and allergen accuracy since 1.1.0, and the owner's rule that organic always earns a benefit. Every cached score is recomputed once (`RULES_REVISION` 2026-09-28.8); nothing is refetched.

**Organic**
- Organic always earns a benefit. That includes organic cane sugar and honey, organic products held at a score cap (organic canola oil scores above conventional), and organic meat and eggs named only in the ingredient list.
- The bare word "organic" is shown as "Organic, unverified". It no longer reads as "USDA Organic". A USDA Organic certification from Amazon's data is "Organic — verified" and counts once.

**Scores**
- **Hidden additives are charged.** A bare "sweetener" or "flavour enhancer" (in each label language) is charged and marks the product ultra-processed. Previously it was free. Neither is charged when the list names the sweetener or enhancer anywhere.
- **Processing class.** The additives that cap the score now also mark it NOVA 4: artificial colours and sweeteners, industrial syrups, trans fat and carrageenan. "Water, sucralose" read NOVA 2.
- **Two ingredients joined by "and"** ("sugar and salt", "azúcar y sal") are read as two when one half is charged. The sugar used to go free.
- **Words read in more languages:**
  - Spanish glucose-fructose syrup ("jarabe de glucosa y fructosa")
  - maltitol and sorbitol syrups, xylitol, stevia and saccharin
  - tocopherols, malted barley, Dutch rice flour and barley, silicon dioxide
- **Facility statements** ("Packaged in a facility that also processes wheat") are cross-contact statements. They raise their allergen flags and are no longer charged as wheat.

**Panel**
- A cap that your goals moved names its default: "caps score at 18 (25 at default weights; your goals weigh it more)".
- The "natural flavors" help says the dial moves points only.

**Tests and CI**
- The corpus calibration failure noted in 1.1.0 (organic-whole-milk at 84) is fixed.
- The browser harness follows the new opt-in defaults.
