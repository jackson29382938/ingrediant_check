# Ingredient Check 1.17.1 — sort by price per any unit

The Amazon *Sort by* menu entry used to mean one thing, price per ounce. It now names the unit
that fits the page, and the side panel can switch to any other. No scoring rule changed:
`RULES_REVISION` stays `2026-10-18.1` and cached scores stay valid.

## What changed

**The menu says what the page is priced in.** The entry reads *Price per ounce: Low to High* on a
cereal search, *Price per gallon* on milk, *Price per count* on snack packs and *Price per 100 g* on the
metric stores. The unit is the one most listings with a unit price use, counting the unit Amazon itself
names ("$4.99/Pound", "$1.25/Count") and otherwise the store's usual unit for the family the listing
belongs to. A tie goes to the family more listings can be priced in, then weight, then volume, then count.
The entry renames itself as results load.

**A Price per picker in the side panel.** *Automatic* follows the same vote. Otherwise pick oz, lb,
100 g, g, kg (weight), fl oz, pint, quart, gallon, ml, 100 ml, litre (volume) or count. The choice lasts for the
open list; *Automatic* resets it. Prices, factor names, headings and the *Prioritize* presets follow the
unit. UK and Irish pints, quarts and gallons use imperial sizes.

**Conversion stays inside a family.** A 32 oz tub and a 2 lb bag compare directly, as do a gallon and a
2-litre bottle. Weight, volume and count do not convert into each other, because a gallon of milk
has no weight in the listing. A listing with no price in the chosen family goes last and says
*No price per …*; the picker shows how many listings each family covers.

**Counts are read.** "Pack of 12", "24 count", "6 ct" and "12-pack" give a per-count price. A title whose pack
and item counts disagree is left unpriced rather than guessed. Count-only listings keep the old per-ounce
field empty, so nothing that read it changes.

**Per serving is extra information.** Where the product reading has "N servings per container", the listing
adds a *$/serving* line next to the main unit. It never sets the ranking.

## Checks

`tests/unit-sort.test.js` covers conversions, imperial sizes, the Amazon unit text, count parsing, the vote and
its ties, serving parsing, the sanitiser and ranking by family. The Chromium harness checks the menu rename,
the panel picker (automatic, per pound, per count, per gallon with an unpriceable listing) and that a
listing keeps its own unit under another default.
