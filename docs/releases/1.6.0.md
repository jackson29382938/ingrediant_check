# Ingredient Check 1.6.0 — search ratings and twelve grocery stores

Target and Walmart searches previously showed only scores for products already opened on the same computer. Retailer previews now reuse cached scores and read uncached public product pages on the same origin when **Rate every search result** is enabled. Readable declarations receive a health score; missing/blocked labels show **Label unavailable**, and disabled/budget-limited scans show **Open to rate**. No default score is invented for an unreadable label.

- Add Instacart, Kroger, Fry’s, Ralphs, King Soopers, Smith’s, Fred Meyer, QFC, Harris Teeter, Dillons, Safeway and Albertsons public product/search adapters.
- Read Target’s public identity-bound ingredient/nutrition declaration, including the exact child TCIN. Fix Walmart overlay-card title anchoring.
- Place Costco badges in title content, or out of flow on image-only cards. Mount the product panel outside the title/wishlist row. Shadow styling protects badge contents from retailer CSS.
- Bound public scans to two concurrent requests, a 15-second timeout and the existing speed budget (20–60 requests). Abort on navigation/settings changes; reject wrong-origin/product redirects, oversized/non-HTML responses and recognized challenges. Stop queued requests on 403/429/503. Requests omit cookies and use no internal retailer APIs.
- Keep scores local; preserve automatic-rating opt-outs. Update popup, settings, setup and privacy copy for the changed network behavior.

## Validation and coverage

All 807 Node tests passed. Regressions cover previously unopened search results, unavailable labels, request deduplication/cancellation, limits, redirect identity, nutrition ambiguity and allergen negation. Chromium fixtures cover first-visit and saved-score search ratings, product panels and late label changes across all fifteen retailer previews. Costco’s image-only regression verifies unchanged card height and placement outside image links. Existing Amazon checks continue to pass.

Ordinary public GETs confirmed the Target chips declaration and oats ingredients/nutrition on all nine Kroger-family banners. Instacart, Safeway and Albertsons returned sampled pages without readable initial declarations; their adapters are tested previews, not claims of complete live shopping support. Store/location hydration and blocked pages can still prevent numeric ratings. See [STORES.md](../STORES.md) and [the audit](../SEARCH-EXPANSION-AUDIT-2026-10-04.md).

## Updating

Download and extract the ZIP. For an unpacked installation, replace files in the **existing extension folder**, then click **Reload** at `chrome://extensions` and refresh shopping tabs. Keeping the folder avoids creating a new extension ID and another Google OAuth redirect. Chrome may ask for access to the twelve added sites. Keep **Show score badges** and **Rate every search result** enabled to scan uncached results.

Google Console redirect registration is separate from this release and remains required for live sign-in.
