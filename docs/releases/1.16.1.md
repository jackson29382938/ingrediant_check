# Ingredient Check 1.16.1 — complete readings and reproducible scores

Unreleased patch based on the published 1.16.0 source (`fef8828`). Existing declared-share, allergen-line, readability, Firefox lifecycle and UI changes are preserved.

## Scoring and evidence

- Nested additive lists retain family relationships and their optional E prefixes. `chocolate (sugar, cocoa butter, emulsifiers (322, 471))` now agrees with `E322, E471` (31, NOVA 4), rather than scoring 56/NOVA 3. Compact `322,471` is also understood in additive-code context; decimal ingredient shares remain decimals.
- Long ingredient entries remain in the assessment, and long/depth-limited readings are explicitly incomplete. A disappearing oil entry can no longer produce an apparently complete score. The shared engine gate declines these readings on every surface.
- Organic claims cannot establish recognition of unknown ingredient identities.
- Declared 100g/100ml quantities use the same comparison basis. Unknown serving mass/volume leaves enabled quantity factors unassessed, retaining ingredient fallbacks. Contradictory mass compositions cannot earn nutrition rewards; sugar/fibre are not double-counted as independent macronutrients.
- Nutrition extraction stops at nutrient boundaries. An absent total-sugar value cannot borrow the protein amount, and `0.125g` remains a decimal. Cold-pressed qualifiers soften only their own oil entry in the supported label languages.
- Sourcing follows the leading unpenalized whole/culinary food rather than a four-entry cutoff. Adding a fifth seasoning no longer removes the sourcing deduction. The representative uncured-bacon fixture changes from 38 to 24 because its five-ingredient pork list is now assessed for sourcing uncertainty; its calibration band becomes 20–65. This is a preference-model correction, not a new health claim.
- Organic secondary ingredients cannot imply organic sourcing of the leading animal food, and the same sourcing claim is not paid twice. Known date varieties remain recognizable without depending on their organic adjective.
- Certification enrichment saves the exact bounded evidence used for the displayed score. Product cache, Orders import and history replay now agree. New snapshots carry an input-schema version; older history comparisons are marked partial without rewriting recorded ratings. Legacy product readings are refreshed once.
- The panel and hover details show enabled-but-unavailable nutrition factors and separate processing, ingredient, nutrition and sourcing contributions. Their displayed components add up to the score.
- Barcode fallback requests explicitly based nutrition amounts and converts sodium units. It uses a complete page or fallback panel, never a mixture of incompatible bases.

`RULES_REVISION` advances to `2026-10-18.2`. Existing score fingerprints expire through the rule revision; routine app-version cache compatibility remains intact.

## Reliability and backend

Delayed settings reads cannot overwrite a newer OCR checkbox change. This has a deterministic regression test; it does not establish that every cause of the previously reported CI click timeout has been eliminated.

The Apps Script template authenticates Google requests before taking the global sheet lock, retaining locked sheet merges, reads and deletions. Request logs include lock wait/hold duration without tokens, account identity or product content. The generated private history schema retains certification evidence. Deploy the updated template separately to use these backend improvements; no production backend is changed by this patch.

## Verification and remaining evidence

Regression checks cover extraction-to-panel reachability, nested-family/format invariance, serving-size equivalence, contradictory nutrition, multilingual qualifiers, evidence serialization, legacy completeness and checkbox races. The review script now asserts corrected behavior instead of reproducing expected defects.

[Current validation](../CURRENT-VALIDATION.md) records executed checks and limitations. [Sensitivity comparisons](../SCORING-SENSITIVITY.md) show the effect of removing processing weight without claiming independent calibration. Neither these checks nor the existing public-label fixtures establish clinical validity. No store publication, signed-in account verification or backend deployment is implied.
