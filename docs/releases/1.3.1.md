A small scoring fix on top of 1.3.0. Cached scores are recomputed once (`RULES_REVISION` 2026-09-28.10); nothing is refetched.

- **Refined flour is charged as refined flour in more wordings.**
  - "Refined wheat flour" and "bleached wheat flour" were charged only as plain wheat flour. They are now charged like "refined flour" and "white flour".
  - The Indian name "maida" is read the same way.
  - "Unbleached wheat flour" is unchanged.
- **A binding agent after a colon names its additive.** In "binding agent: maltitol" (Polish "substancja wiążąca: maltitole"), the maltitol is now charged.
- **E492 is read** like its English name, sorbitan tristearate.

Install: download `ingredient-check-1.3.1.zip` below, unzip it, and load the folder at `chrome://extensions` with Developer mode on (Load unpacked). Goal weights and everything else from 1.3.0 are included.
