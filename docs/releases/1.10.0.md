# Ingredient Check 1.10.0 — cart ratings that work, the cart average, optional points, and a simpler popup and Settings

**Cart ratings, checked on a real Amazon cart.** 1.9.0's cart tests used invented markup, and on a live signed-out cart the scores did not show: the badge went into a hidden "was removed from Shopping Cart" message, and 14 badges landed on the recommendation rails and the page footer instead. Now each item in the active cart shows its score on the title, nothing outside the active cart is touched, and the page Amazon opens right after Add to Cart (`/cart/smart-wagon`) rates its mini-cart line too. Long titles are no longer dropped, a robot-check page is recognised and stops requests, and a cart product is scored from the same evidence as a product page (its labels and certifications and its food check), so a product reads the same number everywhere. Rows inside carousels, suggestions, "buy it again" and footers are excluded on every store. The other 15 stores' cart readers are still checked on fixtures only.

**Cart average.** A line under *Shopping Cart* shows the average of the rated items ("Cart average · Excellent, 6 of 8 items rated"), and the toolbar icon shows the same number on a cart tab. Quantities are not read, so it is the plain average of the lines.

**Optional points.** *Earn points for healthy carts* (Settings → *What to rate*, off by default): 20 XP for an item scoring 90 or more, 10 for 75 or more, each product once a week; level n costs 100 × n XP. It is for fun: points never change a score, stay on this computer, are not part of a backup, and **Reset points** clears them.

**A simpler popup and Settings.** The toolbar popup went from 332 to 88 words (about 1250 to 670 pixels with the page card): the switches that make requests stay on top with a line saying what they send, and the rest sit under *More*. Settings has a sidebar, switch rows with one-line hints that open the longer explanation when clicked, and pill pickers that show the sentence for the one you chose (Standard: 2,518 to about 1,000 words, 7,000 to 4,500 pixels). Every setting is still there. Both pages share one stylesheet, work in light, dark and forced-colors modes, and keep every text at 4.5:1 or better.

Validation: 1011 Node tests, zero-warning lint, preflight, store-ready packaging and the complete unpacked-extension Chrome harness, which now runs the real extension on the real Amazon cart structure and fails if any of the old cart bugs returns. Checked live on amazon.com with four throwaway signed-out carts (one item each, removed afterwards): a visible score on the item, the average and points line, no stray badges, and the post-Add-to-Cart page rated. Retailer challenges and unpublished ingredient labels can still prevent ratings; see [store coverage](../STORES.md#live-check-2026-10-07-amazon-cart).

For an unpacked install, update files in the existing extension folder, Reload at `chrome://extensions`, then refresh store tabs. Keeping the folder preserves the Google redirect address. The release does not change your Google Cloud OAuth configuration. Saved scores are recalculated once after the update.
