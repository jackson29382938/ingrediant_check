# 1.26.1

- **The toolbar popup opens at its full width again.** In 1.26.0 it could open about 210px wide, with every label
  broken to one word per line ("Scored / on / your / computer"). Its width was written as "360px or the window's
  width, whichever is smaller", and the browser sizes a popup's window from its content, so the two measured each
  other and settled on the narrowest the text could go. The popup is now a fixed 360px wide, and its height is left
  to the browser (which scrolls it past 600px) instead of being measured from the window as well.
- **High zoom.** A browser allows a popup at most 800×600 of its own pixels, which leaves less than 360px once the
  default zoom passes about 220%. The switches on the right then sat off-screen behind a sideways scrollbar. The popup
  now takes the width it is given in that case (261px at 300%) and wraps its text to fit.
- Scores, scoring rules and `RULES_REVISION` are unchanged.

Validation: the real toolbar popup opened in Chromium and measured at display scales 1×, 1.5× and 2× (360px wide, no
sideways scroll) and at default zooms of 50%, 200%, 220%, 250% and 300% (fits the window, no sideways scroll); unit tests
(`tests/popup-size.test.js`), preflight, ESLint and the Chromium harness.

Known limits: Firefox's popup was not opened in a browser for this release; it reads the same fixed width.
