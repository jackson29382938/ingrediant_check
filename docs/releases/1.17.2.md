# Ingredient Check 1.17.2

This update improves Amazon comparisons and fixes issues found in the October audit. Food scoring rules retain their existing behavior. The new category indices update the rules revision and retire prior cached scores.

## What changed

- Sort non-food Amazon searches by unit price, and recognize same-day grocery departments and linked result titles.
- Open comparisons follow new Amazon searches by default, with a setting to control that behavior.
- Google backup saves recheck the selected account and consent. Local purchase deletion no longer depends on interactive authentication; remote deletion can remain pending until available.
- Improve cached score retention, retailer badges and breakdowns, order warnings, and purchase history. New purchase records include source information, and sync sends changes in bounded batches.
- Refresh the website and installation guide with feature pages and screenshots.

## Installation

Download the Chrome or Firefox ZIP from the assets below. Firefox installs are temporary unless signed by Mozilla. SHA256SUMS contains checksums for both packages.

The optional Google Apps Script backend must be updated separately to retain the new purchase provenance fields; older deployments can discard them and cause repeated sync attempts.

## Personal care and supplements

- Add separate label indices for toothpaste, mouthwash, deodorant, shampoo, conditioner, body wash/soap, moisturizers and supplements.
- Choose Evidence-based, Minimal ingredients or Preference-focused in Settings; disable these categories independently.
- Show the category and style on product pages and breakdowns, category badges on search/cart items, and separate averages in mixed carts. Mixed categories do not rank against each other by score.
- Preserve Supplement Facts and serving information in local readings. Missing functional ingredients, serving/dose evidence, unreadable lists and unsupported combinations stay unavailable.
- Food score calibration is unchanged. Preference-focused personal-care indices explicitly reflect avoid-list matches; food allergy/diet/avoid warnings retain their existing behavior.
