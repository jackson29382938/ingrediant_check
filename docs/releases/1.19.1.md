# Ingredient Check 1.19.1

Fixes four reported Amazon cart items showing **Label unavailable** despite published ingredients:
Amazon Grocery cut watermelon, Zorrer jujube dates, Navitas pomegranate powder and Amazon Grocery
Original Sandwich Cookies.

- Recognizes published watermelon and jujube ingredient names, including cut/dried forms.
- Keeps actual ingredients after “certified organic” and treats known botanical names in
  parentheses as aliases. Unknown ingredients still reduce coverage.
- Reads encoded HTML line breaks in ingredient fields without dropping the recipe. Contains
  declarations and cross-contact warnings are retained.
- Recalculates fresh saved public labels before requesting a cart product page. A newly saved
  label in another tab can restore an unavailable cart score without reloading.
- When a public Amazon page has no readable text, tries the existing enabled barcode and local
  photo readers. Permission, source switches, product identity and food checks still apply.
  Retailer previews retain their existing behavior. Cart reads are never shared.

The scoring revision advances to retire older calculated answers. The 54 product entries in the
existing scoring corpus retain their default scores. No ingredient list is inferred from a title.

This release also includes the current main-branch grocery-search fixes: badges avoid hidden
quantity alerts, listing titles use the visible product name, and the side panel follows searches
that update without a full page load, including delayed results.
