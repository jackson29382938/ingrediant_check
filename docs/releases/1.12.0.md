# Ingredient Check 1.12.0 — order details and full Amazon history

Purchase scores now overlay product images by default, with a title-placement alternative in
History & sync. Hover or keyboard focus opens saved snapshot details. Point deductions appear
when the local label and scoring setup reproduce the historical score; otherwise the card shows
saved ingredients and warnings without inventing historical deductions.

History & sync adds Scan all Amazon orders: a dedicated ordinary tab visits available year filters
and Next pages, with background mode, Pause, Resume, Cancel and Open scan tab. Progress and a
remaining-time range update from discovered order counts and measured page durations. Chrome must
remain open; sign-in, verification, unreadable layouts and capacity limits pause the scan.
Repeated scans deduplicate purchases and retain existing rating snapshots.

Local history capacity increases to 10,000 entries; scanning is limited to 2,000 pages. The history
table loads 500 entries at a time while trends and exports use all records. Redeploy docs/apps-script.gs
on the existing Google web app for syncing more than 1,000 records. Older deployments continue
working for smaller histories; larger uploads stop before sending tokens or history.

Validation includes safe URL discovery, opt-in and account isolation, forged/replayed report
rejection, pagination/year coverage, estimates, pause/resume, deduplication, and unpacked-Chrome
checks for background navigation, bounded overlays, hover/focus details and placement changes.
Order pages and account responses use controlled fixtures; private live Amazon histories were
not available in the test environment.
