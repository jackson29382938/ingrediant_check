# 1.21.1

Live screenshots of the final rows revealed that separate Walmart result groups can still end with
one product and three empty columns after ad removal. Compaction remains the first choice. The
comparison now fills those residual columns with neutral Ingredient Check summaries, without moving
retailer-owned cards between React containers or covering products.

Ten predefined summaries describe **this loaded search**:

1. Loaded organic products
2. Food ingredient ratings ready
3. Average food ingredient score
4. Highest food ingredient score
5. Excellent food ingredient scores (90+)
6. Lowest listed price
7. Lowest displayed weight-based price per ounce
8. Hidden ad/empty promotional slots
9. Labels still checking
10. Labels unavailable

Unknown values display a dash. Summaries update as real label responses arrive; no purchases,
savings, or personal history are fabricated. Narrower or shorter gaps show fewer cards from this
catalog so summaries fit. Pause, navigation, and a completed row remove the fillers. They create no
extra network requests and preserve the docked sorting panel.

Validated with matched live Walmart screenshots and a Chromium regression reproducing a tall,
single-product row with three leftover columns, all ten summaries, and pause/restoration.
