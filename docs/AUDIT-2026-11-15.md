Audit of the current release: every automated check the repository defines was run, then the scoring
engine was probed with adversarial and regional label formats, the security surfaces were reviewed, and
the extension pages were loaded in a real Chromium with the packed extension. No source was changed.

## Findings to fix first (reproduced engine defects)

All four break the rule in `CLAUDE.md` that silence must not read as cleanliness: a real, rule-listed
ingredient is present and nothing is charged. Each fix moves scores, so each needs `RULES_REVISION` /
`RULES_HASH`, `scripts/scoring-audit.mjs`, release notes and a format case in `tests/invariants.test.js`.

### E1 — P1: "contains not more than 2% of X" erases X

`DENIAL_HEAD` in `src/engine/score.js` treats the `not` / `no` of a quantity phrase as a denial. When the
phrase and the additive share one entry, the whole entry is dropped as marketing.

| Label text (prefix `Water, sugar, citric acid, `) | Score | Charged |
|---|---|---|
| `contains less than 2% of sodium benzoate` | 36 | sugar, **sodium benzoate**, citric acid |
| `contains not more than 2% of sodium benzoate` | **61** | sugar, citric acid |
| `contains not more than 1/2 of 1% of sodium benzoate (to protect taste)` (soda format, with HFCS) | 30 | sodium benzoate missing |
| `Tomato puree, sugar, contains not more than 2% of potassium sorbate` | **65** | sorbate missing |
| `Oats, salt, not more than 2% of: sodium benzoate` | **94** | nothing |

With two or more items after the phrase the later items survive, which is why the corpus did not catch
it. Caps (Red 40) still fire because caps are read separately, so only penalties are lost.
Fix: exclude quantity phrases from the denial head — `not(?!\s+(?:from|more|over|less|to\s+exceed)\b)`
and `no(?!\s+more\b)` — and let the existing lead-in handling strip "not more than N% of". Also check
`not more than 0.1% sodium benzoate (preservative)`, which currently reads only as "Unnamed preservative".

### E2 — P1: "soya oil" (UK/India spelling) is not recognised

The `soybean` rule in `src/engine/db/oils.js` matches `soybean|soya bean|soy` before `oil`, so `soya oil`,
`soyabean oil` and `soy bean oil` fall through as unknown words.

| Text | Score |
|---|---|
| `Water, Oats, Salt, soybean oil` | 63 (seed-oil cap) |
| `Water, Oats, Salt, soya oil` | **80** (unknown entry, coverage 0.75) |

"Soya oil" is the standard spelling on amazon.co.uk and amazon.in labels. `vegetable oils (rapeseed, soya)`
is only charged because rapeseed is. Fix: `\b(?:soy(?:a)?\s?bean|soya|soy)\s+oil\b`.

### E3 — P2: Indian `INS` additive codes are not decoded

FSSAI labels (amazon.in) prefix codes with `INS`. The bare-code path in `expandEntry` reads `colour (102)`
as E102, but `INS 102` is left as text.

| Text | Score | Read as |
|---|---|---|
| `Oats, colour (102)` | 18 | Yellow 5 (tartrazine) cap |
| `Oats, colour (INS 102)` / `(INS102)` | **81** | "Unnamed colour" |
| `Flavour enhancer (INS 627, INS 631)` | 49 | "Unnamed flavour enhancer" |
| `Thickener (INS 1422)` | 74 | "Unnamed stabiliser" instead of modified starch |

Fix in `cleanText`/`tidy`: rewrite `\bINS\s*(?:No\.?\s*)?(\d{3,4}[a-z]?)` to `E$1`, keeping sub-indices such
as `500(ii)` from being read as a bracket.

### E4 — P2: Smaller synonym gaps

| Text | Now | Expected |
|---|---|---|
| `Red-40`, `Yellow-5`, `Yellow-6`, `Blue-1`, `Red-3` | uncharged (`Red 40`, `Red40`, `Red #40` all cap at 18) | dye cap |
| `isoglucose` (EU name for HFCS) | uncharged | glucose-fructose cap |
| `INS 129`, bare `INS 102` | uncharged | dye cap |

Lower priority, calibration questions rather than bugs: `palm kernel oil` and `fractionated palm kernel
oil` score as the good "palm fruit oil" (90), and `Free from: palm oil, soybean oil` charges the soybean
oil it denies (63) — the reverse failure of E1.

## What was checked and found sound

- **Security:** all `innerHTML` writes are static shadow-DOM templates; the one dynamic HTML path
  (`panel.js`) goes through DOMPurify 3.4.16. No `eval`/`new Function`, no `onMessageExternal` or page
  `postMessage` listeners. Worker messages are allow-listed by sender type and host. The offscreen OCR
  fetch checks the URL before and after redirects, the content type and a 10 MB cap. Content-script
  fetches are same-origin with `credentials: 'omit'` where no session is needed. Extension CSP is
  `script-src 'self' 'wasm-unsafe-eval'`.
- **Privacy:** network hosts beyond the stores are optional permissions; API keys stay out of backup keys.
- **Storage:** the score cache is capped (10,000 entries), sheet pulls are size- and row-capped, the
  product cache has age and count limits, and the rule memo clears itself when full.
- **Engine:** 770 format × additive combinations from the probe matched the plain-list result except the
  cases above; E-numbers (`E129`, `E 129`, `E-129`), FD&C names, lakes, and the European languages
  tested (fr, es, it, nl, pl, sv, tr, pt, de) all read and charge the same as English.

## Recommended order

1. E1 and E2 together in one rules revision (largest score error, most common formats).
2. E3 and E4 in the next revision with `invariants.test.js` cases for each.
3. I1 decision (support or stop matching ja/ar marketplaces), then I2/I3.
4. I4–I9 as routine cleanup.
