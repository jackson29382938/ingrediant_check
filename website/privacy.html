Privacy

Summary of the Ingredient Check extension's privacy practices. The full policy, which is what the Chrome Web Store listing uses, is in docs/PRIVACY.md.

The short version

  • Scoring runs on your computer. Scores, settings and the product cache stay in your browser's local extension storage.
  • The extension does not sell data, show ads, or use analytics or tracking.
  • Most features send nothing beyond the page you are already visiting. The optional features listed below are off until you turn them on.
  • This website has no analytics scripts and sets no cookies.

What the extension reads

On supported retailer pages it reads product titles, ingredient lists, prices and the page structure needed to find them, and only what is needed to score the product or show a result.

What stays on your device

Your weights, allergen and diet flags, avoid list, toggles, and a score cache are kept in chrome.storage.local. Product data behind each score is kept for 30 days by default (adjustable from 1 to 90 days). Clear cached scores in the popup removes both. The sorted list and its priorities live in memory only and are cleared when Chrome closes.

Optional features that contact other services

  • Barcode lookup (Open Food Facts). Off unless you grant permission. Only a product barcode is sent, to fill in a missing ingredient list.
  • Public label lookups. When automatic rating is enabled, the extension can request public product pages from the same retailer. These requests omit cookies and referrers.
  • Google sign-in and sync. Only if you use the Google save/restore or purchase-history sync features. These use Google's sign-in and request the openid scope. Tokens are kept in memory only.
  • Purchase history (preview). Only if you turn it on. It reads order pages you open and can sync to a private backend you enable. Addresses, payment details, prices and raw order numbers are not stored or uploaded.
  • Shared product readings. Off by default and experimental.

Reports and store requests

The Report a problem or idea form sends nothing from the extension. You see everything it would include, and you choose whether to download a report file or open an email draft yourself. A screenshot is taken only when you click the toolbar button on an Amazon or retailer tab.

The Request a store form builds an email draft in your own email app. Nothing is sent until you press Send.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not read your browsing history beyond the supported retailer pages.
  • It does not send your settings, products, or account details to us or anyone else.
  • It does not change quantities, prices, totals or checkout controls on carts.
