Know what is in the grocery cart.

Ingredient Check for Chrome and Firefox rates grocery labels 0–100 on ingredients, additives and processing. Choose your goals and weights, see what contributed to each score, and keep your scores on your computer.

Install the extension Read the privacy policy

This is a guide to what is written on the label, not health or allergy advice. Always read the package.

How it works

Score on the product page

On supported food product pages it reads available ingredients and shows a score with a breakdown. Amazon also supports local label-image OCR, permission-based barcode lookup, and pasted labels. Missing or blocked labels stay unavailable.

Scores on search and carts

Supported search results and active carts show ratings from saved labels or available public product pages. Amazon's cart also shows a cart average. Saved-for-later items and recommendations are left alone.

Sort by score or price per ounce

On supported Amazon grocery searches, Amazon's Sort by menu gets two extra options: score (high to low) and price per ounce (per 100 g on metric stores).

Your weights, your rules

Choose how much each factor counts, set allergen, diet and avoid-list flags, and see exactly why a product scored the way it did when you hover over its number.

Optional purchase trends

Enable Amazon purchase history to see order-item ratings, averages, charts and the biggest score contributors. You can scan older orders, filter your records, and compare recorded scores with your current goals.

What it covers

  • Amazon storefronts across 21 countries, with the most complete rules for English US, UK, Canada and Australia labels.
  • 15 retailer previews, including Target, Walmart, Costco, Instacart and Kroger. These are still being validated.
  • Local scoring. Scores and product labels are cached on your computer. Automatic rating, local OCR and certification lookups are enabled by default. Settings backup follows your browser profile sync and permissions; other account and sharing features are explained in setup and privacy settings.

Language support varies. See the full store coverage notes.

Install

Download the current Chrome or Firefox package from the latest GitHub release. These are development packages; the Firefox ZIP requires Mozilla signing for permanent installation.

Chrome

  1. Choose the ZIP whose name ends with the version number and .zip, then extract it into its own folder. The ZIP with -firefox in its name is for Firefox.
  2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
  3. Click Load unpacked and select the extracted folder.
  4. Expect Ingredient Check on the extensions page with no errors. Pin it to the toolbar to see the score for the current product.

Updating later? Replace the files in the same folder and press Reload, so your settings and local history are kept.

Firefox

  1. Choose the release ZIP ending in -firefox.zip and extract it.
  2. In Firefox 142 or newer, open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and select the extracted manifest.json.
  4. Grant site access for the stores you use. Temporary add-ons are removed when Firefox restarts.

See the browser setup guide for signing and Google sign-in configuration. Google sign-in is optional; local scoring works without it.
