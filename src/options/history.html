Purchase history & trends

Only actual Amazon orders drive trends. Viewed products and carts are excluded. Purchase ratings use the ingredient information available when imported, which may differ from the original package. Purchases do not tell us what you ate.

Import and account sync

Sync sends product names and IDs, purchase dates, ingredient text, ratings and selected warning matches to your authenticated account record in Ingredient Check's private backend. No addresses, prices, payment details or raw order numbers are stored. History is separate from settings backups.

Order score display

Image placement falls back to the title when an item has no product image; title placement keeps an overlay for image-only items. Hover details use the saved purchase rating.

Label reading and speed

Reads ingredient text from product label images on your computer. Photo reading can take longer than text or saved ratings; it never guesses nutrition numbers.

Only the public product barcode is sent to Open Food Facts. Your orders are not sent there.

Saved ratings always appear first. Photo reading has its own queue. Amazon may refuse faster requests; blocked requests stop.

Establish your Amazon history

Scan all available Orders pages and year filters using your Amazon sign-in. This opens one ordinary Amazon tab; it can work while you use other tabs. Chrome must stay open. Login checks or unsupported pages pause the scan. Imported purchases are kept when you pause or cancel.

Initial estimate: roughly 15–60 seconds per page of about 10 orders. Cached labels can be much faster; missing labels and photo reading can take several minutes per page. The remaining-time range updates after pages finish and more years are discovered.

Only confirmed purchases contribute to trends. Quantities, addresses, payment details and raw order numbers are excluded. Up to 10,000 distinct order/product entries can be kept locally. Older account backends need the updated history capacity to sync more than 1,000 entries.
