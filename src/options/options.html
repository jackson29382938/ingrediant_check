Ingredient Check

Everything below runs on your computer. No account, no AI, no data leaves this machine except the two optional lookups you switch on yourself.

What to rate

These settings are the same ones in the toolbar popup. Changing them here updates scores on Amazon immediately.

Skips shoes, toys, cosmetics, medicine and cleaning products. A score on shampoo would imply it was judged as something you could eat. When this is on, a non-food page shows why it was skipped instead of a silent blank.

Starting point

What counts, and how much

1.0 is the default weight. 0 switches a category off entirely. 2.0 makes it matter twice as much. Categories set to 0 by default are ones people reasonably disagree about — turn them on if you want them.

Allergen warnings

Flagged on the panel. These never change the score — whether a food contains sesame is a different question from whether it is a good food.

Diet compatibility

Your own avoid list

One ingredient or phrase per line. Anything matching gets flagged on the panel.

Shared provenance database

For single-ingredient foods — ground beef, eggs, milk, salmon — the ingredient list tells you almost nothing. Everything that varies is in certifications, and Amazon publishes very little of it. A shared sheet lets that be recorded once per product instead of re-derived by everyone. Leave both boxes empty and the extension stays entirely offline.

Only product identifiers and certification text are ever sent. Never anything about you or what you browse, and never without this box ticked.
