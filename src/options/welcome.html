How much do you want to set up?

This decides how many settings you see, here and in Settings. Nothing is locked: change it any time, and settings you do not see keep sensible defaults.

What are you aiming for?

Pick goals that fit your preferences. They combine; overlapping priorities are averaged according to how much each goal counts. Change those priorities later in Settings. Goals guide ingredient scores; they do not predict weight loss or medical outcomes. Pick nothing for the balanced default.

Anything to warn you about?

Tap any allergens or diets. Warnings sit on top of the score and never change it. They only cover the ingredient text on the page — always check the package.
